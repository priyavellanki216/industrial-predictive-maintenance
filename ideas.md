# ForgeSense — Design Direction

## Three Possible Approaches

### 1. The Signal Workshop
**Very Brief Intro:** A refined engineering editorial with material-heavy factory imagery, calibrated technical typography, and precise data marks. It feels like a world-class lab notebook made for operations teams.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. The Glass Control Room
**Very Brief Intro:** A pale, architectural interface where dashboard elements float in light, quiet space. It communicates clarity, safety, and analytical rigor rather than machine drama.

**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. Midnight Operations
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark operations environment that treats sensor signals as a living industrial nervous system. Subtle illumination, high contrast, and machine-scale imagery create focus without visual noise.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Approach: The Signal Workshop

### Design Movement
The project takes cues from **Swiss industrial design, engineering editorials, and modern scientific instrumentation**. The visual experience should feel engineered rather than decorated.

### Core Principles

1. **Evidence before ornament:** data, status, and pipeline concepts lead; decoration only gives them texture and context.
2. **Material honesty:** graphite, steel, paper-white surfaces, clean hairlines, and sparse calibrated colour feel like a credible industrial tool.
3. **Asymmetric narrative:** layouts create a guided reading path around machinery, data samples, and operator insights rather than placing everything in centered card grids.
4. **Calibrated urgency:** teal signals confidence and healthy operation while the amber accent is reserved for meaningful anomaly states.

### Color Philosophy
The base is a layered range of **warm off-white, graphite, and iron grey** that references machinery, field notebooks, and operational calm. **Mineral teal (#0D8D84)** is the signature colour: a credible diagnostic signal rather than generic tech blue. **Safety amber (#FF9E42)** appears only where the interface speaks about risk, thresholds, or predictions that deserve attention.

### Layout Paradigm
The page is an **instrument panel laid across a field report**. A broad cinematic machine image breaks across the top, followed by offset modules which alternate between editorial copy columns, full-width data bands, and framed diagnostic cards. The visual rhythm should feel like the reader is moving through a predictive-maintenance decision from sensor to action.

### Signature Elements

1. **Calibration rulers:** small monospace readouts, hairline separators, and section labels are placed like physical instrument annotations.
2. **Signal threads:** fine teal waveform strokes link distant areas of the page and trace the logic from raw sensor input to a prediction.
3. **Offset evidence cards:** thin-border, sharp-to-soft cards overlap imagery and section edges like reviewed engineering documentation.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interaction is purposeful and measured. Hovering a pipeline stage reveals its tooling and output, metric chips clarify their definition, and the live prediction panel changes state only in response to a conscious user action. Controls should feel as precise as a physical instrument.

### Animation
Motion is restrained and diagnostic. Signal strokes drift subtly over a 6–8 second interval, content enters with 16–24px vertical movement and opacity over 420ms using a crisp ease-out, and buttons compress to 97% on press. Alert pulses are used sparingly and only around the active anomaly indicator. All nonessential motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
**Space Grotesk** supplies headings and large numeric readouts; its angular construction feels engineered and authoritative. **Manrope** carries body copy with generous line-height for calm legibility. **IBM Plex Mono** is reserved for labels, model metrics, sample IDs, and system states. Headlines are compact and decisive; body copy remains conversationally technical.

### Brand Essence
**ForgeSense turns noisy machine telemetry into a clear, deployment-ready signal for teams that cannot afford unplanned downtime.**

Personality: **precise, composed, field-tested.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines make a concrete operational promise. CTAs sound like an inspection instruction, not marketing language; microcopy labels confidence, ownership, and action.

> “Detect the failure pattern before the shift feels it.”

> “Run a sample prediction.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark pairs a compact **turbine–pulse–core** symbol with a bold, slightly tracked “FORGESENSE” wordmark. The mark suggests a motor under observation, with a protected core at its center. The symbol stands alone in the navigation and as the favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Forge Teal — #0D8D84.**

## Style Decisions

- **Global mood rule:** ForgeSense may use dark graphite sections, but the dominant impression remains an engineering field report and instrument panel. Every dark area must include material hairlines, calibrated labels, or evidence readouts.
- **Motif rule:** Calibration rulers, sample IDs, mono status labels, and fine teal signal threads form one continuous instrumentation layer across every major section.
- **Brand rule:** The turbine–pulse–core mark functions as a recurring inspection stamp and certification symbol, in addition to the navigation logo.
